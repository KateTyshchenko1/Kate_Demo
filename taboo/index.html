Sex, porn, and technology

Taboo

I work in sex tech. Nearly every day, I watch banks, payment processors, social platforms, ad networks, app stores, and most VCs treat us as if our work is wrong or even borderline illegal.

The irony is that the sex industry keeps helping technology move forward.
